# Design assets

## Original hero

- Workspace asset: `frontend/public/images/rentalk-living.jpg`
- Generated using the built-in image-generation tool, then converted to JPEG for web delivery.
- Used in the homepage and authentication layout, both with an explicit AI-generated/illustrative caption.
- It is not a photo of a listed property.

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial hero photograph for renTalk, a refined Vietnamese home-rental website. Create an inviting contemporary apartment living room in Ho Chi Minh City, tastefully attainable rather than extravagant: cream linen sofa, low warm oak coffee table, travertine floor, an olive accent lounge chair, tall leafy indoor plant and sheer white curtains beside large windows looking toward soft tropical foliage and distant city buildings. Architectural magazine photography, authentic natural textures, warm soft morning sunlight and subtle shadows, calm lived-in feeling, uncluttered. Wide horizontal 3:2 composition, eye-level 24mm perspective with the sofa in the center and window to the right, plenty of room context. Restrained ivory, oak, sage and deep forest palette. No people, no text, no logos, no watermarks. High-quality realistic photography, not a 3D render.
